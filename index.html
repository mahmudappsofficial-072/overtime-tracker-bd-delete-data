How to Delete Your App Data Without Deleting Your Account

Overtime Tracker BD allows you to delete your app records without deleting your Google Account or Firebase Authentication account.

Open the Profile/Settings section of the app, select Delete All Data, review the warning, and confirm your choice.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When the deletion process completes successfully, the following data will be deleted or reset:

  • All overtime records.
  • All piece-rate records.
  • Monthly salary settings.
  • Advance and other deduction records.
  • Records stored in Trash.
  • Worker name, company name, designation, Worker ID, and grade.
  • Basic salary, house rent, medical, conveyance, and food allowances.
  • Attendance bonus and related bonus settings.
  • Night bill rate, tiffin bill rate, and custom overtime rate.
  • Monthly overtime target.
  • Support WhatsApp number.

These records are deleted from the device's local database and the corresponding Cloud Firestore collections when cloud deletion succeeds.

What Data Will Not Be Deleted?

Delete All Data does not delete your Google Account or Firebase Authentication account.

The following information is not deleted by this feature:

  • Your Google Account and Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Your Privacy Policy acceptance record and consent timestamp.
  • Cloud-stored Privacy Policy consent audit information.
  • PDF pay-slips previously exported or downloaded to your device.
  • Backup copies managed through Android Auto Backup or Device Transfer.

Internet and Google Sign-In Requirements

When signed in with Google

An internet connection is required to delete your cloud records. Once cloud deletion succeeds, the corresponding Firestore records and local database records are cleared. The app then signs you out.

Without an internet connection

If cloud deletion fails, the app will not clear the local database either. Restore your internet connection and try again.

When not signed in with Google

Only the data stored in your device's local database will be deleted. Any previously stored cloud data will not be deleted through this process.

Important Notice

Delete All Data is a permanent operation. Successfully deleted records cannot be restored through the app. Please export any important records as PDF before proceeding.

This feature does not delete your Google Account. You may sign in again and continue using the app.

  • Firebase User ID (UID)।
